PCB Design

Low Voltage Disconnect PCB.

A battery protection board designed to cut off voltage below a threshold.

GitHub files Back to projects

This PCB was built for a Collaborative Lab Design class project. Its purpose was to disconnect the load when rechargeable batteries dropped below their indicated safe voltage range.

The design focused on protecting the batteries from being drained too far, keeping the system safer and more reliable during use.
