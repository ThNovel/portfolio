PCB Design

Low Voltage Disconnect PCB.

A battery protection board that disconnects the load when voltage falls below a safe threshold.

GitHub files Back to projects

This PCB was developed as part of a Collaborative Lab Design project to protect rechargeable battery systems from excessive discharge.

The board continuously monitors battery voltage and automatically disconnects the load once the voltage drops below a predefined threshold. This prevents the batteries from operating outside their recommended range, reducing the risk of permanent damage and improving long-term reliability.

The project involved schematic capture, PCB layout, component selection, and hardware validation, resulting in a compact and practical protection solution suitable for battery-powered systems.
