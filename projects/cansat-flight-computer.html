PCB Design

Cansat Flight Computer PCB.

The brain of our Cansat, built to stay plug-and-play and easy to debug.

GitHub files Back to projects

This board was designed as the flight computer for a Cansat, a satellite-style system built in the size of a can. The main goal was to keep the board practical: plug-and-play connectors, straightforward debugging, and less painful soldering.

The PCB worked as expected and gave the team a cleaner path for testing the rest of the system. It was a successful board-level prototype with a focus on usability, reliability, and fast iteration.
